01 / Profile

Executive
Summary.

Research philosophy, foundational principles, and cross-disciplinary trajectory.

Md. Tanvir Hasan is a computer science engineering researcher and systems architect working at the intersection of Deep Learning, Explainable AI (XAI), Medical Image Analysis, and Distributed Compute Infrastructure.

His investigations focus on solving high-stakes challenges where algorithmic opacity carries real-world consequences: developing interpretable hybrid CNN-Vision Transformer frameworks for clinical screening, knowledge distillation for resource-constrained edge devices, and carbon-aware GPU scheduling pipelines for high-performance computing clusters.

With 16+ peer-reviewed book chapters, conference proceedings, open research datasets, and manuscripts under review, his work couples rigorous empirical methodology with practical, open-source software engineering. Alongside his academic research, he leads distributed AI infrastructure platform engineering at Daffodil International University (ClusterOne DevTeam).
